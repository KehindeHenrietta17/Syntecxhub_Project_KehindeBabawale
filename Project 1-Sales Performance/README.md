## Insights

This dashboard provides an overview of the company's sales performance across orders, products, regions, and time.

**Key Performance Metrics**

- Processed 6,000 customer orders, generating $1.57M in total revenue.

- Achieved a total profit of $175.26K, representing an overall growth rate of 77.29%, indicating strong business expansion.

**Sales Performance**

- Sales increased consistently throughout the year, with Q4 recording the highest sales, suggesting strong seasonal demand.

- Year-over-year revenue grew from approximately $0.56M in 2019 to $1.00M in 2020, demonstrating significant business growth.

**Category Performance**

- Technology emerged as the highest revenue-generating category, contributing approximately 41% of total sales.

- Office Supplies and Furniture accounted for the remaining sales, highlighting a well-diversified product portfolio.

**Regional Insights**

- The West region generated the highest sales, contributing over one-third of total revenue.
- Sales across the remaining regions were relatively balanced, indicating healthy market coverage with opportunities to strengthen lower-performing regions.
- 
**Product Performance**
  
- Several products consistently generated the highest sales, making them key revenue drivers that should remain a focus for inventory and marketing strategies.
- The least-performing products contributed minimal revenue, presenting opportunities for promotional campaigns, pricing adjustments, or product rationalization.

**Monthly Trends**

- Monthly sales fluctuated throughout the year but accelerated significantly during the final quarter, peaking in December.
- The trend suggests increased customer demand during the holiday season, reinforcing the importance of strategic inventory planning and promotional activities during peak periods.

**Business Recommendations**

- Continue investing in Technology products, as they are the primary revenue contributors.
- Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of Q4 to capitalize on seasonal demand.
- Improve the performance of underperforming products through promotions or portfolio optimization.
- Investigate successful strategies in the West region and replicate them across other regions to drive overall growth.

## Project Summary

This dashboard was developed in Power BI to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights using interactive visualizations, KPIs, and trend analysis. The analysis enables stakeholders to monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and make informed, data-driven decisions.
