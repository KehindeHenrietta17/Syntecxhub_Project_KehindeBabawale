## Insights

This interactive Power BI dashboard analyzes student academic performance across Mathematics, Reading, and Writing while exploring how demographic characteristics and test preparation relate to overall achievement. The project involved data cleaning, modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard design to transform raw student records into actionable insights.

**Key Insights**

- Students achieved an average overall performance of 61.18%, indicating moderate academic achievement across the three core subjects.
- The highest-performing student scored 78.00%, while the lowest scored 43.67%, revealing a performance gap of over 34 percentage points and highlighting opportunities for targeted academic support.
- Mathematics recorded the highest average score (62.51%), followed by Writing (60.53%) and Reading (60.49%). The small variation suggests relatively balanced performance across all subjects.
- Students who completed the test preparation course achieved an average score of 66.34%, compared to 57.59% for those who did not, making test preparation the strongest factor associated with improved academic performance in this dataset.
- Average performance across parental education levels ranged between approximately 59% and 63%, indicating only modest differences and suggesting that parental education had a limited influence on student outcomes.
- Performance across ethnicity groups was similarly consistent, with average scores clustered around 60–63%, indicating relatively equitable academic outcomes.
- Male and female students performed almost identically (61.08% vs. 61.26%), suggesting that gender had little impact on overall academic achievement.
- The Top 5 students, ranked by Overall Percentage, demonstrated consistently strong performance across Mathematics, Reading, and Writing, while also highlighting individual subject strengths among high achievers.

**Conclusion**

The analysis suggests that while overall student performance is satisfactory, there is room for improvement, particularly among lower-performing students. Among all variables analyzed, test preparation showed the strongest association with higher academic performance, emphasizing the value of structured preparation programs. Meanwhile, demographic characteristics such as gender, ethnicity, and parental education exhibited relatively small differences in performance, suggesting that academic support initiatives should focus more on learning interventions than demographic factors.
